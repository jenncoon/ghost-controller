Case Briefing: Operation Clean Sweep

Coon Consulting Services · Fraud Investigation Unit

Listen up, Team. We've just been called in by the Board of Directors at Coon Consulting Services. Their lead controller, "Fast" Eddie Ledger, vanished last night.

Before he left, Eddie sent a cryptic email claiming he had "cleaned the books" and that the company's equity was far lower than reported. He didn't delete the data — but he did something worse: he left the accounting cycle unfinished.

The Board needs to present their final, audited numbers to the bank in exactly 60 minutes, or their credit line will be frozen.

Your Mission: Step into Eddie's office, reconstruct June's activity, and find the true Retained Earnings balance. If your final number matches the bank's records, we've caught Eddie in a lie. If not… the company is bankrupt.

How this works:

  • 1Work through 5 phases in order. Each phase builds on the last — you cannot skip ahead.
  • 2Use the scaffolding fields in each phase to work out your answers on screen.
  • 3Enter the Vault Code at the bottom of each phase. Numbers only — no dollar signs or commas.
  • 4If your code is wrong, you will receive a targeted hint. You have unlimited attempts — keep working until the vault opens.
  • 5Complete all five phases to close the case and submit your grade.

Enter your name before beginning. It will appear on your Cycle Master badge if earned.

Required. Your name will appear on your badge if earned.
